# Privacy Policy — Cafe Restaurant Management System

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**App Name:** Cafe Restaurant Management System  
**Developer:** Hamza Zehri

---

## Overview

Cafe Restaurant Management System ("the App") is a point-of-sale and restaurant management application designed to run locally on your device. This privacy policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## Data Collection

**The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties.**

All data entered into the App — including menu items, orders, customer information, employee records, financial transactions, and settings — is stored **exclusively in a local SQLite database** on the user's own device.

---

## Data Stored Locally

The App may store the following types of data on your device:

- **Business Information:** Restaurant name, logo, currency symbol, tax rates
- **Menu Data:** Food/drink items, categories, prices, cost prices, images
- **Order Data:** Orders, order items, invoices, payment methods, discounts
- **Customer Data:** Customer names, phone numbers, credit balances
- **Employee Data:** Staff names, roles, attendance records, salary payments
- **Financial Data:** Cash register transactions, expenses, cash in/out
- **App Settings:** Printer configuration, floor/table layouts, license information
- **License Data:** Machine ID and activation key (used for offline license validation only)

---

## Data Storage & Security

- All data is stored in a local SQLite database file (`pos.db`) within the user's Documents folder
- No data is sent to any external server, cloud service, or third party
- No internet connection is required for the App to function
- The App does not use analytics, tracking, or telemetry services
- Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes on the local device only

---

## Internet Usage

The App does not require an internet connection. If an internet connection is available, it may be used solely for:

- Verifying license activation keys (offline validation is also supported)
- Checking for application updates (if enabled by the user)

No user data is transmitted during these operations.

---

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

---

## Data Deletion

Users can delete their data at any time by:

- Deleting the local database file from the App's data folder
- Uninstalling the App (which removes all local data)
- Using the "Reset Database" option in the App's settings

---

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
